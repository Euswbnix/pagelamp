This is the first pre-release of PageLamp v0.3. It is for people who want to try it and tell us
what goes wrong. It isn't finished and it can have bugs. If you'd rather wait, stay on v0.1.0,
the latest stable release: https://github.com/Euswbnix/pagelamp/releases/tag/v0.1.0

The README has the setup: https://github.com/Euswbnix/pagelamp#readme
The CHANGELOG has every change: https://github.com/Euswbnix/pagelamp/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md

## Read this first

- Your data moves to a new format that v0.1.0 can't open. A copy of the old database is kept.
  See "Your data after the upgrade" and "Going back to v0.1.0" below.
- PageLamp now syncs by itself while it is open. You can turn that off.
- It reads more of a Canvas course than v0.1.0 did, and Canvas may record that. See "What
  PageLamp reads from Canvas" below.
- It looks for a new version on GitHub once a day. You can turn that off.
- PageLamp still calls no AI model itself. Your own AI app reads your courses, as before.
- The native macOS app in the repository (apps/macos) is not part of this release. The macOS
  download is the same desktop app as on Windows and Linux.

## What's new since v0.1.0

- Automatic sync. While PageLamp is open it syncs by itself: twice a day, unless you choose
  once a day or off under "Sources & sync" (command line: pagelamp sync --auto off). With
  nobody at the app it asks Canvas only for your course list, deadlines and announcements. When
  you open PageLamp, or come back to its window and click, type or scroll, and the last full
  sync is old enough, it runs a full sync, the same as pressing Sync. It never downloads Canvas
  files by itself, nothing syncs while PageLamp is closed, and after you stop a sync nothing
  starts by itself for an hour.
- PageLamp looks for updates. See "Updates from this version on" below.
- Weeks that follow the real class dates. A Canvas term that is much longer than the classes
  is no longer used to count weeks. Each course shows its week, "Exams", "Ended" or when it
  starts, and its Timeline tab lists the dates PageLamp used, the ones it didn't, and why. Still
  wrong? Set the first and last day of classes under "Course dates" on that tab. A course that
  is over, inactive or hasn't started shows no current week.
- Past courses. Courses that have ended, or that have no dates and nothing new for months, move
  to a collapsed "Past courses" group; their deadlines stay listed. "I'm still taking this"
  moves one back.
- More of a Canvas course, and a list of what wasn't read. PageLamp now reads a course's Home
  page and the pages its texts link to. A Canvas course's page lists what PageLamp didn't read
  and why ("What PageLamp didn't read", at the end of "This week").
- Stop a sync. "Stop" ends a sync after the file it is on; what was synced so far is kept.
- Sturdier file reading. Each file is read in a separate process with time and memory limits,
  so one bad PDF can't stop a sync, and a file whose text can't be read says why.
- Cleaner link addresses. A link address in a Canvas page can carry a parameter that opens a
  file for whoever has the address. PageLamp now removes such parameters from the link
  addresses in the text it stores, and before it gives text to your AI app.
- For your AI app. A new read-only tool, list_materials, lists every material of a course.
  sync_status says whether PageLamp syncs by itself and how old each source's data is. Your AI
  app is told what PageLamp didn't read, and that it can't start a sync and must not try.
- A new look for the desktop app, with "Reduce transparency" under Settings, Appearance.
- Command line. pagelamp courses groups courses into Current, Upcoming and Past (past ones with
  --past or --all). New: pagelamp course timeline, pagelamp course keep, and pagelamp sync
  --auto with off, daily or twice-daily.

## What PageLamp reads from Canvas, and what Canvas may record

Canvas may record a sync like any other access to a course. As far as we know, the course's
access report, which instructors can see, may list the areas PageLamp reads (modules, pages,
assignments, files, announcements) and the pages it reads, and a sync may refresh the "Last
Activity" time shown for you in the course. We can't see Canvas's records, so we can't say
exactly what it keeps. With automatic sync on, a full sync can run without you pressing Sync:
when you are at the app and the last full sync is 12 hours old (24 with "Once a day").

Compared with v0.1.0, a full sync of a Canvas course now:

- also reads the page the course's Home shows, and the pages of the same course that the Home
  page, the syllabus, other pages and announcements link to. One step only, and at most 40
  linked pages and 150 linked files per course in one sync. Linked files are listed, not
  downloaded.
- asks for the announcements of the last 365 days (v0.1.0: 120).
- no longer reads a page that a module asks you to view, until you have viewed it in Canvas. As
  far as we can tell, reading it for you could mark it as viewed. In two cases PageLamp can't
  know beforehand: the first time it reads a course's Home page, and when a link uses another
  address of such a page. If it opened one, the sync says so, and it doesn't read that page
  again until you have viewed it. v0.1.0 read every page of a module.
- reads some pages again. A page Canvas lists with a change date is read only when it is new or
  has changed. A page with no change date (the Home page, and linked pages in a course that
  doesn't show its Pages list) is read again each time you press Sync, and by an automatic sync
  at most once a day. Once after this update, an unchanged page is read again in a course that
  doesn't show its Files list, to find the files it links to.

PageLamp only reads: GET requests with your own token. It never asks for a list the course
hides. Assignments and quizzes stay title, due date and link. Files are downloaded only when
you ask.

With nobody at the app, an automatic sync makes no request for a course's modules, pages, file
list or assignments. Canvas keeps its own records, and we can't promise that these lighter
requests leave none. To sync only when you press Sync, set automatic sync to "Off".

What PageLamp didn't read is listed with a reason: on the course's page, on the source's row
after a sync, in pagelamp sync, and for your AI app.

## Downloads

- Desktop app (it includes the pagelamp command your AI app needs):
  macOS: PageLamp_0.3.0_universal.dmg, one file for Apple silicon and Intel.
  Windows (x86_64): PageLamp_0.3.0_x64-setup.exe. It installs for your account only; there is
  no .msi any more.
  Linux (x86_64): the .deb or the .rpm (they also install /usr/bin/pagelamp), or
  PageLamp_0.3.0_amd64.AppImage, which runs the app, but your AI app can't use the pagelamp
  inside it.
- Command line only: pagelamp-v0.3.0-alpha.1-<platform>.tar.gz (aarch64-apple-darwin is an
  Apple silicon Mac, x86_64-apple-darwin an Intel Mac, x86_64-unknown-linux-gnu is Linux) or the
  .zip for x86_64-pc-windows-msvc (Windows). Each holds pagelamp, LICENSE,
  THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.txt and README.md.
- Installer names say 0.3.0, and so do the .deb and .rpm package versions. The app says
  0.3.0-alpha.1 (Settings, About, or pagelamp --version).
- The .app.tar.gz, .sig and latest.json files are for the updater; you don't need them.
- Checksums: SHA256SUMS lists every installer, archive and updater file. Check with
  shasum -a 256 -c SHA256SUMS --ignore-missing
  Each of those files also has a GitHub build attestation; how to check one is in
  https://github.com/Euswbnix/pagelamp/blob/main/SECURITY.md

## Installing (also over v0.1.0)

v0.1.0 doesn't update itself and won't offer this version, so install it by hand. Quit PageLamp
and your AI app first.

- macOS: open the .dmg, drag PageLamp into Applications (choose "Replace" if v0.1.0 is there)
  and open it from there. The app, the .dmg and the command-line pagelamp are signed with a
  Developer ID and notarized by Apple; macOS asks once whether to open an app downloaded from
  the internet. Be online for that first run.
- Windows: run the -setup.exe. The installer, the app and pagelamp.exe are signed, so Windows
  shows a verified publisher; SmartScreen may still warn while this release is new: "More
  info", then "Run anyway".
- Windows, if you installed v0.1.0 from the .msi: the new installer is made to remove that
  installation first, and Windows may ask for an administrator's permission for it. Afterwards
  pagelamp.exe is in another folder, so copy the setup from "Connect your AI app" into your AI
  app again. We haven't walked through this path by hand; please tell us how it went.
- Linux: install the .deb or the .rpm, or make the .AppImage executable and run it. Builds are
  not code-signed; check them against SHA256SUMS. Saving a calendar-feed link or a Canvas token
  needs GNOME Keyring or KWallet.
- If your AI app runs a pagelamp you installed on its own (from an archive, or with cargo):
  replace that file with this version's too. Once your data is in the new format, v0.1.0's
  pagelamp can't read it and asks you to update PageLamp at every call.

## Your data after the upgrade

- The first time this version opens your data (the app, the pagelamp command or your AI app's
  connection, whichever comes first), it moves the data to a new format. Before that it copies
  the old database to pagelamp.db.v2.bak, next to pagelamp.db. The copy holds the same course
  data. It is deleted when you remove your last source. Where the data folder is:
  https://github.com/Euswbnix/pagelamp/blob/main/PRIVACY.md
- Your sources, courses, AI-policy settings and study plan are kept. Your Canvas token and feed
  link stay in the system keychain.
- Course dates you set in v0.1.0 ask you to check them once. Ones that only repeated the synced
  term dates are cleared.
- Link addresses in the stored text lose their access parameters (see above). PageLamp does
  this before it makes the copy.
- A file that became locked or was moved in Canvas, and hasn't been read again, no longer shows
  its old text.
- "What's new" is shown once. Automatic sync and the update check start only after you close
  it, and you can turn both off right there.
- Quit and reopen your AI app, so it starts the new pagelamp. An AI app that still runs
  v0.1.0's pagelamp can't read the new format.

## Updates from this version on

- Once a day PageLamp downloads a small file from GitHub to see whether there is a new version.
  GitHub sees your IP address and your PageLamp version, as with any download; nothing about
  your courses is sent. The first check waits until you have seen this explained in the app.
  Turn it off under Settings, Updates; "Check now" still works.
- A new version is shown at the top of every page. Nothing is installed until you choose
  "Install and restart", and PageLamp installs only an update whose signature it has checked.
  It won't install during a sync.
- Afterwards, quit and reopen your AI app so it uses the new pagelamp; PageLamp reminds you.
- A copy installed from the .deb or the .rpm gets a link to the new version's page instead.
- This version follows the Beta update channel, which also gets every stable release.

## Known limits

- Updating in the app is new. If an update fails, install the new version by hand from
  https://github.com/Euswbnix/pagelamp/releases and tell us.
- Linux wasn't tried by hand for this release: the three Linux packages are built and checked
  by the same automation as the others, but nobody installed them and went through a sync.
- Stay on the Beta channel. No stable release has update information yet (v0.1.0 has none); on
  "Stable", a check says so and finds nothing.
- Installer names and the .deb and .rpm versions say 0.3.0 for every 0.3.0 pre-release.
- No reading weeks or breaks. They can't be set in this version, and PageLamp doesn't read
  dates from a syllabus, so after a break the week shown can run ahead. Exam dates can't be set
  either: the three weeks after the last day of classes count as exams.
- Past courses keep syncing, and this version can't remove a single course. To keep one away
  from your AI app, hide it (the course's Settings, "Hide this course").
- Automatic sync runs only while PageLamp is open. There is no background mode.
- Links are followed one step, within limits. What was left out is listed under "What PageLamp
  didn't read".
- The "asked to view" rule rests on how we read Canvas. We haven't tested on a real account
  that reading such a page marks it as viewed.
- If antivirus software blocks PageLamp's file reader, PDFs and other files over 5 MB aren't
  read until it can run; Settings, "Help & feedback" says when that happens.

## Going back to v0.1.0

v0.1.0 can't open the new data format. To go back:

1. Quit PageLamp and every AI app that uses it.
2. Install v0.1.0 again from https://github.com/Euswbnix/pagelamp/releases/tag/v0.1.0
3. In the data folder, move pagelamp.db aside (and pagelamp.db-wal and pagelamp.db-shm if they
   exist), then copy pagelamp.db.v2.bak to pagelamp.db.
4. Open PageLamp and sync. Then open your AI app again.

Changes made after the upgrade are lost. This needs pagelamp.db.v2.bak: it is missing if the
copy couldn't be made, or if you removed your last source after upgrading. A diagnostic report
says whether the copy was made ("Last database update").

## Uninstalling, and deleting everything

Uninstalling removes the app, not your data: the course database, downloaded files, the backup
copy and the logs stay in the data folder, on every system. How to delete all of it is under
"Delete everything" in https://github.com/Euswbnix/pagelamp/blob/main/PRIVACY.md
