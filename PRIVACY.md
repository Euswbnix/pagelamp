# Privacy

*Last updated: 2026-09-26 · applies to StudentOS v0.1*

StudentOS is a local app. There is no StudentOS server, account, analytics or telemetry. The people
who build StudentOS never receive your data.

## What StudentOS stores, and where

| Data | Where | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Course list, modules, material titles and text, announcements, deadlines, your study plan, your per-course settings (AI policy, AI access, term dates, hidden) | a SQLite database in your data folder (macOS `~/Library/Application Support/dev.StudentOS.StudentOS`, Windows `%APPDATA%\StudentOS\StudentOS\data`, Linux `$XDG_DATA_HOME/studentos`, or `STUDENTOS_HOME`) | so your AI app can answer questions about your courses |
| Files you ask StudentOS to download from Canvas | the `files/` folder next to the database | so their text can be indexed |
| Canvas access token, calendar-feed link | your operating system's keychain (macOS Keychain, Windows Credential Manager, Linux Secret Service) — never in the database, logs or AI output | to sync on your behalf |

StudentOS does **not** store assignment instructions or submissions — only assignment titles, due
dates and links. It never reads your university password.

## What leaves your computer

- **Sync (only when you start it):** StudentOS connects to your LMS (read-only requests with your
  own token) and/or downloads your calendar feed. Course folders are read locally.
- **When you ask your AI app a question:** your AI app (Claude, ChatGPT, Codex, …) reads the course
  information it needs from StudentOS on your computer and sends it to that AI provider **under your
  own account and that provider's terms**. What the provider stores or uses for training depends on
  your account settings with them — check them.
- Nothing else. StudentOS never contacts any other server.

## Your controls

- **Per course:** turn off *"Let my AI app read this course's materials"*, or mark the course's AI
  policy as "No AI" — StudentOS then shares no material text for that course (deadlines, structure
  and your study plan remain available for planning). CLI: `studentos course ai-access <course> off`.
- **Hide a course** to keep it out of your AI app entirely: `studentos course hide <course>`.
- **Remove a source** (`studentos sources remove <id>` or *Sources & sync → Remove*) deletes its
  courses from the database and its secret from the keychain.
- **Delete everything:** quit StudentOS and delete the data folder above.

## AI disclosure

StudentOS does not generate content itself in v0.1. Text your AI app produces is AI-generated by
that app. StudentOS tells your AI app to cite course sources, tutor rather than produce graded work,
and respect each course's AI policy — but you are responsible for following your course and
university rules.
